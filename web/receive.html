How it stays private →

Connected to an older peer using transfer v2 automatically. This transfer lacks v3 receiver credits and transport-candidate authentication. Receive buffers remain bounded; a fast legacy sender and slow disk may exceed them. Upgrade the older peer for v3 protections.
